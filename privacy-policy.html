Privacy Policy — BoredomBustersPublisher

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who we are

BoredomBustersPublisher is a personal-use application operated by Merry Maker (Parareach Ltd). It is not a public service: it has exactly one user — the account owner — and connects only to the owner's own Pinterest business account via the official Pinterest API v5.

2. What the application does

The application publishes original content created by the owner (illustrated kids' activity pins promoting the book Boredom Busters) to the owner's own Pinterest boards, on a schedule of one pin per day. It reads the owner's board list and creates pins on the owner's behalf. That is all it does.

3. What data the application accesses

  • The OAuth access token issued when the account owner authorized the app. It is stored only on the owner's own machine and is never shared with anyone.
  • The owner's own content: board names, pin images, titles, and descriptions.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or process any other person's data.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not access, read, or store any data from Pinterest accounts other than the owner's.

5. Data retention & deletion

The only persistent data is the access token and a local log of which pins were published, both held on the owner's machine. Deleting the application and revoking token access in Pinterest settings removes all access immediately.
